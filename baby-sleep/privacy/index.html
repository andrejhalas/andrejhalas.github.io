About Baby Sleep

Baby Sleep is an offline application designed for parents and caregivers to play sleep sounds and create their own short audio recordings.

Microphone and audio recordings

The app requests microphone access only when the user deliberately starts the custom recording feature. A custom recording:

  • is created on the user's device,
  • is processed locally on the device,
  • is stored in the app's local storage,
  • is not sent to the data controller,
  • is not sent to advertising or analytics platforms.

Users can delete their custom recordings directly in the app. Android backup and device-to-device transfer are disabled for app data.

Account

Baby Sleep does not require a user account.

Advertising and tracking

The app:

  • does not contain advertising,
  • does not use advertising identifiers,
  • does not contain a separate analytics SDK,
  • does not contain a separate crash-reporting SDK.

The data controller uses aggregated statistics and diagnostics available through Google Play Console and Android Vitals. Google may collect this information according to the rules and settings of Google and Android services; Baby Sleep does not send it to the data controller's server.

Purchases

Lifetime Premium is a one-time digital unlock distributed through Google Play Billing. Google Play processes payments and payment details. Baby Sleep receives the information needed to determine whether the user owns the relevant product and stores the unlock status locally for offline use. Google's processing of information is governed by Google's own terms and policies.

Retention and deletion

Local app data remains on the device until the user deletes it or uninstalls the app. Google Play manages the purchase record. The Restore Purchase feature may verify ownership of Lifetime Premium again.

Children

The app is intended for parents and caregivers, not for children as independent users.

Security

The app minimises data transmission and keeps personal recordings locally. However, no system can be described as completely secure.

Changes to this policy

If the way data is processed changes or a new SDK or service is added, this policy will be updated before the relevant version is released.
