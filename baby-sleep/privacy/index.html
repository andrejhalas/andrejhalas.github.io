About the app

Hush, Little One (Búvaj krásne) is a baby sleep sounds app for parents and caregivers. It includes standard soothing sounds and lets you record or import your own audio. Standard sounds can be played without an account, without ads, and offline.

Microphone, recordings, and imported audio

The app requests microphone access only when you deliberately start recording. Recordings and audio files you choose to import:

  • are created or selected by you on your device,
  • are processed and stored locally in the app,
  • are not sent to the developer, advertising services, or analytics services.

You can delete your custom audio in the app. App data is excluded from Android backup and device-to-device transfer. Your recordings and imported audio are not restored when you restore a Premium purchase.

Accounts

The app does not require you to create an account or sign in.

Advertising, analytics, and diagnostics

The app contains no ads, does not use advertising identifiers, and has no separate analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting SDK. Google Play may make aggregated usage statistics and crash diagnostics available to the developer through Play Console and Android Vitals, depending on Google and Android service settings. Google processes information for these services under its own terms and privacy policies; the app does not send this information to a developer-operated server.

Premium purchases

Lifetime Premium is a one-time, non-renewing digital purchase made through Google Play Billing. Google processes the payment and payment details. The app receives the purchase information needed to check whether Premium is unlocked and stores the unlock status locally so it can be used offline. Google Play manages the purchase record. Restoring a purchase restores the Premium entitlement, not audio files that were stored only on your device. Google's handling of purchase information is described in Google's Privacy Policy.

Storage and deletion

Recordings, imported audio, and app settings remain in the app's local storage until you delete them or uninstall the app. Android backup and device-to-device transfer for app data are disabled. Purchase records are managed by Google Play; the app's Restore Purchase feature may contact Google Play to check your Lifetime Premium entitlement.

Children

The app is designed for parents and caregivers, not for children to use independently. It does not ask users to submit children's personal information.

Security

The app is designed to minimise data transmission and keep personal audio on your device. No method of storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Your privacy rights

Depending on the applicable data protection law and the information concerned, you may have the right to request access to, correction or deletion of personal data, to restrict or object to certain processing, or to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. To contact the developer about privacy, email ahdigitallabs@gmail.com. For information processed by Google Play, please also refer to Google's privacy information and account controls. You may contact the Office for Personal Data Protection of the Slovak Republic.

Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change or a new SDK or service is added, this policy will be updated before the relevant app version is released.
