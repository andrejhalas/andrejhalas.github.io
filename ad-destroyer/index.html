A popup smashing game with a useful side

AD DESTROYER is a free browser game about the internet’s most irritating interruptions. Every target is an original, fictional advertisement. Hammer through six waves, shred with a chainsaw, or use a laser to face the Cookie Consent Monster and the Unskippable Video Ad.

Outside the arena, find practical guides to fake virus warnings, a popup-blocker test, and interactive dark pattern examples. The game does not change your browser settings, remove malware, or destroy real ads. It just gives your frustration somewhere satisfying to go.
